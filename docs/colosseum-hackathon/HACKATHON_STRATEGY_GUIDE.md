# Colosseum Hackathon & Accelerator Strategy Guide

Source: Colosseum Official Guide (How to Win a Colosseum Hackathon)
Focus: Sivan Payment Ai on Solana

================================================================================
CORE PRINCIPLES FOR WINNING COLOSSEUM
================================================================================

1. Think Like a Venture Capitalist (Colosseum is a Venture Firm)
Colosseum hackathons are not school science fairs or tech experiment bounties. They are founder scouting accelerators designed to deploy capital into viable, high-growth startups.
- Focus on real customer demand, unit economics, and recurring transaction velocity.
- Emphasize why Solana is fundamentally required (sub-second finality, micro-gas costs, non-custodial x402 facilitator rails).

2. The 3-Minute Golden Rule for Demos
- Judges review hundreds of projects. Presentations must be concise, crisp, and self-evident.
- Never spend 2 minutes explaining general blockchain theory. Open directly with the painful problem and immediately show the live working product.
- If a judge has to pause or struggle to understand what the product does, you lose.

3. Deliver the Aha Moment in Seconds
- The Aha moment for Sivan: Creating a verified service agreement inside a Telegram chat and having funds settle autonomously on Solana with zero app download.
- Show, do not tell. Live screen walkthroughs beat slide decks every time.

4. Build in Public & Show Real Traction
- Colosseum judges heavily evaluate team velocity and community proof.
- Share weekly updates on X (Twitter), tag @Colosseum, and document the build journey.
- Onboard real beta testers during Weeks 2 and 3 so the final submission includes real transaction numbers, testimonials, and volume.
